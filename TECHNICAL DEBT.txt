UPDATE status=COMPLETED -> trigger INSERT shell (uncommitted di A).
Lalu panggil generateInvoiceForEncounter yang buka tx B (conn B) ->

pakai flow sequential yang aman.

atau (b) DROP Express legacy di Phase 4 (rekomendasi).
